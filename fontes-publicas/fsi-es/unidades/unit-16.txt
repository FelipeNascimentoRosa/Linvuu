          The 2 ·3 forms of the indirect clitic pronouns are ambiguous in their reference, sinee they can correspond to any of three subject pronoun forms. lE this ambiguity

         The redundant construction consists of the relator  /a/     plus an appropriate nonclitic pronoun. It has cOllsiderable freedom of occurrence in a sentence, appearing

 unstressed and are not emphasized by a shift in word stress as is usual in En¡¡;lish; emphasis is ¡¡;iven by the addition of a redundant phrase, which can appear under strong
 stress and /or a higher pitch. Thus, 'He sends me the newspaper' would be /memánd? /elpery6dl.ko¡/ but 'He sends me the newspaper' would be /ami ¡me
