Notes to the Basic Sentences 



The infinitive form of many verbs can be used as a noun in German. It is 
always a das -noun. English has similar nouns, like playing , working , 
speaking , etc, but often uses the infinitive in equivalent sentences. 

This adjective always has an ending of some kind, in this case the ending 
that the specifier-adjective-noun sequence den anderen Zimmern requires. 
There is no endingless citation form, but regular adjective endings are 
added to the stem ander-. 



The verb stellen "to put" is complemented either by a prepositional phrase 
such as an die Wand or by the word hin. It does not occur without a 
complement, however.
