Helpful hint: in a dictionary, irregular verbs are marked as irreg . They are usually listed in the back of the dictionary, with their irregular forms (some verbs are irregular in the past tense, but not in the present tense, etc.). False cognate warning: in English you take a picture , but in German you make them: Fotos machen . And if you say Fotos nehmen , that just describes that you pick them up and run off with them ... very impolite.
