But instead I have to see her every day ... sob-sob-sob Irregular verbs form the participle by completely remodeling the infinitive form of the verb, and sadly – unlike regular verbs – there is no rule or guide as to how to form the irregular participles (hence the name "irregular"). You simply need to memorize irregular verbs; the only similarity is the ge- prefix and the -en suffix: 1) take the infinitive treffen (meet) 2) remove the -en suffix treff 3) change stem vowel (as necessary) troff 4) add ge- prefix getroff 5) replace -en suffix getroffen Irregular verbs that (can) have a direct object OR that do not denote an action that results in a change of state or being use haben as the auxiliary verb to form the conversational past.
