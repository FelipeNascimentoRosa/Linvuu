However, nicht and kein are not interchangeable. Kein Kein is used to negate a noun that would otherwise be preceded by an indefinite article or by no article at all. But by no means does the queen want to adopt a child! She prefers to wait. Nicht Because kein is a type of article, it is used specifically with nouns. In contrast, nicht is an adverb, so it is used to modify many different parts of language, including: verbs, adjectives, other adverbs, prepositions, phrases, clauses, and sentences.
