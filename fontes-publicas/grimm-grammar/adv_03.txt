They modify verbs or adjectives or other adverbs. They are also used to connect thoughts (and sentences) to form longer narratives, such as establishing a chronological sequence or cause-and-effect relationships. For chronological accuracy, German relies more heavily on adverbs than on verb tenses. People in Germany will be quite confused if you misuse adverbs.
