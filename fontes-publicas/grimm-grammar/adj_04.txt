Most often unpreceded adjectives, like the name suggests, have no article (or determiner) before them. The term 'unpreceded' may be a misnomer, however, since adjectives can be preceded by such determiners as einige , ein wenig , ein bisschen , ein paar , einige , etwas , viele , wenige or numbers (e.g., 20, 100) and still take the so-called strong endings.
