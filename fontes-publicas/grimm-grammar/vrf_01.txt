When the object of the verb is not the same as the subject of that action (the person or thing who performs the action), the sentence has a subject, a verb, and a separate (direct) object. Reflexive verbs in the past tense and past subjunctive Reflexive verbs in the simple past follow regular, irregular and mixed patterns and need to be memorized. In the conversational past and in the past subjunctive , they always take haben as an auxiliary verb (because by definition they always have an object: the self).
