Whether to use a definite or an indefinite article in German is very similar to how you would use them in English: Your browser does not support the audio element. Ein Frosch ist ein Tier. A frog is an animal. Der Froschkönig will eine Prinzessin küssen - irgendeine Prinzessin!!! The frog king wants to kiss a princess - any princess!!! As in English, there is no indefinite article in German in front of plural nouns because there is no plural form of the indefinite article (i.e., just like in English you couldn't say " a kings" or " a dwarves," you can't say " ein Könige" or " ein Zwerge" in German). Thus, you just let the noun stand on its own: Your browser does not support the audio element. Könige haben Königreiche . Kings have kingdoms. Zwerge gibt es oft in Märchen . There are often dwarves in fairy tales.
