It is usually introduced by a determiner that helps identify whether the noun is singular or plural. Die Bäume geben dem Wolf gute Deckung. The trees give the wolf good cover. There are many ways that German can form plurals, and there is no really easy way to guess which noun has which plural ending, so it's best just to memorize the plural forms along with the gender of each noun. The two key things that happen to nouns when they are made into plurals is that they may receive an ending (e, n, en, er, s) and they may receive an Umlaut on their stem vowel (main vowel of the noun). Since there is little pattern to the distribution of endings (although there are some), it is best to work backwards and group nouns according to the changes they go through in the plural. Don't worry, though, you do this automatically in English as well, although there are many different ways to form English plurals too!
