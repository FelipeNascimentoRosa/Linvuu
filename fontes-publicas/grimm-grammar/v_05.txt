Although there are some patterns as to how these prefixes affect the verbs' meaning, for the most part you just need to learn the individual verbs. The inseparable prefixes stay connected with the verb at all times: be -, emp -, ent -, er -, ge -, ver - and zer -. If it helps you, think of the prefix and the verb as connected but separate entities: the main part of the verb (without the prefix) is conjugated the same way as the original verb.
