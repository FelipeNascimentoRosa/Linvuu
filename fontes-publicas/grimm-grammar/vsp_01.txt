For the most part, it is used in formal and literary written texts (e.g., a novel, a police report, newspaper articles). North Germans often use the Präteritum for certain verbs even when they speak (e.g., modal verbs, sein, haben, wissen). Southern Germans would most likely use the Perfekt .
