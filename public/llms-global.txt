# Linvuu (International) — https://linvuu.com

> Structured online language courses for English speakers, from zero to B1: Brazilian Portuguese, Russian and French, explained in English. Written by Felipe Nascimento Rosa and Luiza Nascimento Rosa.

## How it works
- Each lesson is split into 30-minute sessions: listen and read, understand the rule compared with English, practise with instant correction.
- Audio for sentences, words and dialogues.
- The first 2 lessons of every course are free, no card required.
- A path from zero (A1) to B1 of the Common European Framework of Reference.
